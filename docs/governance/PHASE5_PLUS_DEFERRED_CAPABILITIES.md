# Phase 5+ deferred capabilities

`MASTER_REGENT=TCDX_GRC_MASTER_REGENT_BASELINE_v1.7_2026-09-23`

This register classifies findings from the 2026-09-29 Phase 5+ precheck. It authorizes no implementation or new intermediate phase. Phase assignments come from rector 43; Phase 5 remains the Compliance, Controls, Evidence and Actions slice.

| Need | Evidence from Phase 5+ review | Rector phase | Dependency | Why deferred |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Risk heatmaps, KRI and aggregate risk scoring | Control/assessment detail invites risk summaries, but no Risk result can be inferred from a Control mapping | 6 — Risk + Incidents/Loss | Governed risk methodology, measurements and incident/loss slice | Phase 5 control assessment does not establish risk status |
| Integrated multi-framework Audit workflow | Crosswalks and control relations can suggest audit groupings | 7 — Integrated Audit | Approved Audit criteria, scope, team, competency and crosswalk selection contracts | An audit engagement and its SoD workflow are a separate slice |
| Third-party, resilience and autonomous privacy workflows | Subjects and evidence can later support these domains | 7 — Third Parties + Resilience + Privacy | Each domain's persistence→runtime gate | Phase 5 Subject selectors grant no authority to operate these workflows |
| Connector-driven scoring and automated rule impacts | Evidence and assessments may later consume external observations | 8 — Data/Integration/Rules/Impact | Provider contract, source lineage, data quality and calculation gates | No Phase 5 mapping or evidence link may become a calculation or automated decision |
| Advanced analytics, aggregate dashboards and Report Studio | Basic Phase 5 screens need clear drill-down but not aggregate cross-domain results | 9 — Reporting, Regulatory Intelligence and IA | Governed metric definitions, data sufficiency and reporting contracts | Phase 5 must not calculate official scores from incomplete local UI data |
| Regulatory recommendations and IA assistance | Catalog relations can support suggestions later | 9 — Regulatory Intelligence and IA | Licensed provenance, backend integration and human oversight | AI cannot grant entitlements, decide applicability or assert compliance |

The basic Phase 5 catalog, search, filters, applicability, control implementation, assessment, evidence and actions remain in Phase 5 and are not deferred by this register.
