## Versioned successor path proposed for later exact approval

1. Keep the two canonical global immutable registries, ComplianceMethodology and ControlEffectivenessMethodology. Do not update their published v1 rows or alter historical assessments. A successor would use a distinct version_number and its compatible immutable FormulaDefinition; proposed version 2 is a design label, not a created row or allocated operational identifier.
2. Obtain a PO authored rules document per methodology/version with population, evidence validity/sufficiency/absence, recency, provenance, overrides, source resolution, confidence and status propagation. Preserve the rules already published in 17/19. Pin each rule to exact source/version/hash and canonical subject/context; a manifest with empty rules is not sufficient.
3. Use existing MetricDefinition and MetricDefinitionInput contracts where the input is an ObservationType or metric dependency. Their input_code, input_role, is_required/is_blocking and ordinal express input declarations; they do not themselves establish Methodology-to-MetricDefinition applicability. That missing typed relation cannot be replaced by equal names, shared formula UUIDs, rector_source text, first/latest rows, JSON flags or a new side registry.
4. The next canonical amendment must decide the exact methodology/input-definition relation and the immutable mode declaration before physical/API/consumer changes. Two materially different representations remain possible: an approved typed relation among the existing versioned definitions, or a formally approved extension of a canonical versioned declaration. Neither is implemented here. D1=B authorizes this design, not either schema nor their unpublished hashes.
5. Publish a successor only after official input rules, canonical relation, immutable source/version binding, exact delta manifest, PO acceptance and applicable gates. If completeness requires EC, keep the contract's compatible EC path; only a fully bound self-sufficient version may declare none. No new configurations, numbers, legal terms, aliases or time windows are selected here.

## Required acceptance and tests

Reproduce official arithmetic; denominator/applicability; evidence absence/expired/rejected/sufficiency; recency boundaries with exact supplied values; provenance and conflicting sources; wrong-domain/tenant/source/version; immutable published rows and historical results; publication replay/hash corruption; EC compatibility and no inference; complete sufficiency and mode binding.
